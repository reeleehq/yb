yb.download.ranges¶

Download just a time range of a video: parse start/end, build yt-dlp’s options.

Sourcing a sound effect or a clip from a long video should not mean downloading the whole thing. start= / end= on yb.download.download_youtube_video() and yb.download.download_youtube_audio() take seconds (7.9) or timestamps ("0:07.9", "1:02:03"); this module turns them into yt-dlp’s download_ranges option.

>>> parse_timestamp("1:02.5")
62.5
>>> TimeRange.of(start="0:07.9", end=8.75).label
'7.9-8.75'
>>> TimeRange.of(start=30).label
'30-end'

yt-dlp cuts a range by handing the stream to ffmpeg, so the range path needs ffmpeg on PATH (or extra_opts={"ffmpeg_location": ...}). Cuts land on keyframes unless precise_cuts=True (re-encodes, slower, frame-accurate). When the system ffmpeg is a build that lacks what yt-dlp asks of it, point ffmpeg_location at another one (moviepy’s bundled ffmpeg, for example).

yb.download.ranges.ranged_filename_template(template, time_range, *, precise_cuts=False)[source]¶

template with the range before the extension: "T (id) [7.9-8.75].mp4".

Without it a clip would share the full video’s filename, and yt-dlp would take one for the other (“already downloaded”). A precise cut is named apart from a keyframe cut for the same reason. Only templates ending in .%(ext)s (the default does) are changed; one written by hand is the caller’s to keep distinct.
